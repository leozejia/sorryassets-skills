# Pixar

## Identity
Stylized 3D animation with warm, rounded, appealing forms and expressive
character acting. Use for family-friendly, emotionally warm, character-driven
pieces that need approachability rather than realism.

## Palette
Warm-biased, saturated but controlled. Complementary accents (warm skin against
cool environment). Bright midtones, gentle contrast, no crushed blacks.

## Light
Soft key with generous fill; low lighting ratio (2:1–3:1). Warm key
(3200–4500K) with subtle cool bounce. Signature: soft rim light separating
rounded subjects from painterly backgrounds; global-illumination softness.

## Composition and Lens
Clear staging with strong silhouettes. Slightly wide, gentle lens; shallow but
soft depth of field. Eye-level for characters, subtle low angle to make heroes
appealing. Uncluttered frames that read instantly.

## Camera Movement
Smooth, motivated, gentle. Slow push-ins on emotional beats; floating dolly and
crane. Avoid handheld shake and jarring moves.

## Texture and Material
Clean stylized 3D render: soft subsurface skin, plush fabrics, rounded edges,
slightly exaggerated proportions. Polished but not photoreal; no grain.

## Prompt Anchors
- "stylized 3D animation, Pixar-like character design"
- "soft global illumination, warm key light with cool bounce"
- "rounded appealing forms, expressive large eyes, clean silhouette"
- "shallow soft depth of field, gentle rim light"
- "polished 3D render, subsurface skin, plush textures"

## Avoid
Photorealism, harsh shadows, desaturated grim palettes, film grain, gritty
texture, extreme wide-angle distortion, horror lighting.
