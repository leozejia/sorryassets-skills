# Commercial final review scaffold

Review the playable assembled commercial against the brief. The pass bar is
commercial, not narrative. Use `references/craft/defects.md` to classify flaws.

Inputs:
- brief, single message, CTA, delivery constraints: {{contract}}
- product fidelity anchors: {{colors, logo, proportions, material}}
- message plan and shot decisions: {{plan and selections}}
- playable output, probe, manifest, and graph: {{final evidence}}

Report:

1. **Message clarity**: is the single message unmistakable to a first-time
   viewer with no context? If a viewer could name a different takeaway, the
   message is not landing.

2. **Product fidelity**: across every shot, are the product's colors, logo
   placement, proportions, and material accurate to the reference? List any
   shot where fidelity drifts. A fidelity break blocks acceptance even if the
   shot is attractive.

3. **Conversion close**: does the final beat carry or clearly set up the CTA
   (the desired viewer action)? Brand text/logo is composited in editing, but
   the visual must leave room and intent for it.

4. **Craft**: framing, pacing, and camera moves suit a commercial (clean hero
   framing, motivated moves, appropriate style). Transitions match intent (see
   `references/craft/cinematography.md`).

5. **Defects**: for each flaw, state the defect type from
   `references/craft/defects.md`, the shot and approximate timestamp, and the
   severity. Confirm no shot required legible generated on-screen text.

6. **Delivery and evidence**: runtime, dimensions, codec, local path, source
   order, digest, tasks, charges, and lineage agree with the manifest.

7. **Verdict**: accept or reject. State the strongest moment, the weakest
   moment, and the single smallest evidence-based correction if the execution
   boundary permits another attempt.

The commercial fails when the message is ambiguous, product fidelity breaks, the
close does not drive the action, or a blocking defect is present. Do not average
shot scores into a passing verdict.
