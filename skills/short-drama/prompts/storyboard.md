# Shot-plan scaffold

Break the script into the smallest coherent ordered shot sequence that meets the
runtime delivery constraints.

Script: {{script}}

Cast and continuity: {{cast}}

Production bible and available references: {{bible and selected asset nodes}}

Delivery constraints: {{duration, aspect, resolution, and other limits}}

For each shot, provide:

- narrative purpose and target duration;
- start state and end state for character, prop, location, and screen direction;
- framing, camera behavior, lighting, setting, and time;
- continuous visible action and dialogue/sound needs;
- entry/exit transition and its relationship to adjacent shots;
- required reference assets mapped to supported binding roles;
- visible continuity anchors and explicit pass/fail criteria;
- one self-contained generation prompt after the shot is ready.

The total planned duration must match the delivery target. Do not add shots that
do not advance the story or clarify the intended result. Flag any shot whose
action, continuity, transition, or inputs are not yet ready instead of writing a
plausible prompt around the gap.
