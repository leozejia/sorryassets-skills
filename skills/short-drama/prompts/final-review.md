# Final film review scaffold

Review the playable assembled film against the approved brief and production
evidence. Do not infer quality from provider success or from isolated stills.

Inputs:

- brief, delivery constraints, and quality bar: {{contract}}
- story spine, script, and shot plan: {{narrative plan}}
- production bible and take decisions: {{continuity and selections}}
- playable output, probe, manifest, and graph: {{final evidence}}

Report:

1. Narrative legibility: intended promise, causal progression, decisive change,
   and resolution are readable without hidden planning context.
2. Continuity: identity, location, props, lighting/color, screen direction, and
   intentional state changes survive every join.
3. Cinematic craft: framing, action, camera, pacing, transitions, and motifs
   support the story instead of adding unrelated motion or effect density.
4. Defects: list any identity mutation, anatomy break, flicker, spatial jump,
   text artifact, dead action, incoherent transition, or distracting technical
   flaw with its exact moment.
5. Delivery and evidence: runtime, dimensions, codec, local path, source order,
   digest, tasks, charges, lineage, and selected takes agree.
6. Verdict: accept or reject, the strongest moment, the weakest moment, and the
   smallest evidence-based correction if the current execution boundary permits
   another attempt.

A film fails when a required story, continuity, craft, delivery, or evidence
gate fails. Do not average independent shot scores into a passing verdict.
