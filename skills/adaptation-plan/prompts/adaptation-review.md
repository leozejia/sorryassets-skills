# Adaptation review scaffold

Judge the plan as a producible whole, not as a synopsis. Use the drivers and
output structure in `references/craft/adaptation.md` as the contract.

Inputs:
- source map and beat inventory: {{nodes}}
- format decision and episode-count justification: {{...}}
- character map, structure, per-episode cards, adaptation notes: {{plan}}
- per-episode briefs: {{nodes}}

Report:

1. **Episode-count soundness**: is the count derived from the drivers, inside
   the format envelope, and justified — not arbitrary? Flag any bloated or thin
   episode.

2. **Even weighting**: does each episode carry roughly equal story weight? Name
   the lightest and heaviest episodes.

3. **Hooks**: does every episode boundary land on an unresolved question or
   reversal? List any boundary that rests instead of hooking — each is a defect.

4. **Spine**: does the single dramatic question run through all episodes? Point
   to any episode that does not advance or complicate it.

5. **Adaptation integrity**: are interiority, exposition, POV, nonlinear time,
   and subplots handled with named choices? Are the cuts recorded so the creator
   can see what left the source and why?

6. **Character map completeness**: does every recurring character have identity
   anchors sufficient for `character-assets` to lock them across episodes?

7. **Brief runnability**: pick two episode briefs and check each is genuinely
   self-contained enough to run in `narrative-short` without the rest of the
   plan — premise, beats, characters with anchors, effect, and delivery
   constraints all present.

8. **Verdict**: accept or reject. The weakest episode and the single change that
   would most improve the plan.

The plan fails when the episode count is arbitrary or padded, a boundary lacks a
hook, the spine breaks, the character map is too thin for production, or a brief
is not runnable on its own. Do not average.
