# Wong Kar-wai

## Identity
Saturated neon-lit urban intimacy with step-printed motion and a melancholic,
nostalgic mood. Use for romantic longing, memory, loneliness in the city, and
emotionally interior pieces.

## Palette
Highly saturated warm/cool contrast: amber and jade greens, deep reds, teal
shadows. Rich, film-like color; dense blacks with color bleeding into them.

## Light
Low-key, practical-driven (neon signs, lamps, windows). High ratio (6:1+) with
pools of colored light and deep shadow. Warm tungsten mixed with cool
fluorescent; motivated colored sources rather than clean studio light.

## Composition and Lens
Frame-within-frame through doorways, mirrors, grilles, and foreground clutter.
Tight, intimate framing; obstructed views. Longer lens compression; shallow
depth isolating a face in a busy environment.

## Camera Movement
Step-printing (dropped frames with motion blur) for dreamy stutter. Slow,
observational; handheld intimacy; lingering on a subject after action ends.

## Texture and Material
Film grain, halation and bloom around lights, reflective wet surfaces, smoke and
atmosphere. Rich, tactile, slightly hazy.

## Prompt Anchors
- "saturated neon-lit interior, amber and teal color contrast"
- "step-printed motion, motion-blur stutter, dreamy slow motion"
- "frame within frame through a doorway, obstructed foreground"
- "shallow depth of field, longer lens compression, isolated face"
- "film grain, halation bloom around practical lights, hazy atmosphere"

## Avoid
Clean high-key light, flat neutral color, wide clear establishing shots, sterile
digital sharpness, symmetrical product-style framing.
