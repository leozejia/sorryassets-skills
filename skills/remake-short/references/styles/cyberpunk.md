# Cyberpunk

## Identity
Neon-noir near-future urbanism: high-tech, high-contrast, rain-slick and
reflective, with a cool synthetic bias. Use for sci-fi, tech, edgy product, and
dystopian-cool pieces.

## Palette
Cool-dominant (electric blue, cyan, magenta, violet) punctuated by hot neon
accents. High saturation on light sources against near-black surroundings.

## Light
Low-key, high ratio (8:1+). Hard colored neon and holographic sources; strong
rim and edge light on subjects. Cool base temperature with magenta/cyan
practical accents. Volumetric haze catching light beams.

## Composition and Lens
Dense vertical cityscapes, layered depth, foreground signage. Low angles
emphasizing scale and dominance; anamorphic-wide feel with horizontal flares.
Reflective surfaces doubling the neon.

## Camera Movement
Slow deliberate pushes, drifting crane moves through layered environments,
occasional drone-like descents. Controlled, not chaotic.

## Texture and Material
Wet asphalt reflections, rain, holographic overlays, chromatic aberration,
lens flares, atmospheric fog. Glossy, reflective, high-tech surfaces.

## Prompt Anchors
- "neon-noir cityscape, electric blue and magenta neon against near-black"
- "rain-slick reflective streets, volumetric haze, light beams"
- "hard rim light, holographic signage, anamorphic lens flares"
- "low angle, dense vertical city, layered depth"
- "cool synthetic color grade, chromatic aberration"

## Avoid
Warm pastoral daylight, soft high-key, flat matte surfaces, rural/natural
settings, low-contrast neutral grading.
