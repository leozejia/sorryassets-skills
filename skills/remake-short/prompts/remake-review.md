# Remake review scaffold

Judge the remake **against the original**, side by side, using the recipe card
as the contract. Use `references/craft/defects.md` to classify flaws.

Inputs:
- recipe card (intent, mechanisms, timeline, invariants, slots): {{card}}
- fidelity level: {{逐帧复刻 / 格式复用 / 意图移植}}
- subject fidelity anchors: {{anchors}}
- playable output, probe, manifest, and graph: {{final evidence}}
- overlay script and music direction text node: {{node id}}

Report:

1. **Invariants preserved**: go through the recipe card's invariant list one by
   one — present and intact in the remake? Any broken invariant blocks
   acceptance at the 逐帧复刻 and 格式复用 levels.

2. **Mechanism lands**: watch as a first-time viewer. Does the stated emotional
   mechanism actually fire (the incongruity reads, the reference is
   recognizable, the punchline hits at the planned moment)? State the moment
   where it lands — or where it dies.

3. **Subject fidelity**: is the substitute recognizably the creator's own
   (their cat's coat pattern, their product, their face)? Check every shot
   against the fidelity anchors. Drift here defeats the entire point of the
   remake.

4. **Structure match** (逐帧复刻 only): shot count, order, durations, and
   punchline timing match the original's timeline within tolerance.

5. **Defects**: for each flaw, the defect type, shot and timestamp, severity.

6. **Overlay plan completeness**: every planned text has wording, in/out
   timestamps, position, tone; the music direction names the track or a
   concrete mood spec with its entry cue. The plan alone should be enough for
   the creator to finish in one editing pass.

7. **Delivery and evidence**: runtime, dimensions, codec, local path, source
   order, digest, tasks, charges, and lineage agree with the manifest.

8. **Verdict**: accept or reject. The weakest moment, and the single smallest
   correction that would most improve the remake if another attempt is
   authorized.

The remake fails when an invariant breaks, the mechanism does not fire, the
subject stops being recognizably the creator's, or a blocking defect is
present. Do not average.
