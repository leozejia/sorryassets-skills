# Production bible scaffold

Turn the approved brief and story spine into a compact visual and continuity
contract for the whole production.

Brief and story spine: {{brief and causal progression}}

Delivery and execution constraints: {{format, authority, budget, catalog}}

Record:

1. The audience promise and visual grammar in concrete traits, without relying
   on an artist, studio, franchise, or genre label as a substitute for direction.
2. Each character's immutable identity anchors and intentional state changes.
3. Each recurring location's layout, scale, time, weather, and lighting anchors.
4. Each story-bearing prop's appearance, ownership, position, and state changes.
5. Palette, contrast, texture, lens/framing, camera-motion, and screen-direction
   rules that carry across shots.
6. Recurring visual or sound motifs and where they transform or resolve.
7. Known failure modes or forbidden drift that would break recognition, space,
   causality, tone, or delivery.
8. An asset/reference plan mapping every reusable or shot-specific asset to its
   production purpose, selection criteria, and a supported binding role.

Separate invariants from intentional changes. Make every continuity rule
visible enough to inspect in a still, take, transition, or final film.
