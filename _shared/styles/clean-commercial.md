# Clean Commercial

## Identity
Bright, high-key, product-forward advertising look with minimal distraction and
premium polish. Use for product demos, brand pieces, and conversion-oriented
commercial short video.

## Palette
Clean and controlled: bright neutrals, generous white, one or two brand accent
colors. High key, high value, low clutter. Color subordinated to the product.

## Light
High-key, low ratio (2:1 or softer). Large soft sources; bright even fill;
gentle gradient falloff on seamless backgrounds. Neutral to slightly cool
temperature. Crisp specular highlights that make product surfaces read as
premium.

## Composition and Lens
Product-forward with abundant negative space. Centered or rule-of-thirds hero
framing. Clean seamless or minimal set. Macro and detail inserts for texture and
craftsmanship. Shallow depth to isolate the product.

## Camera Movement
Smooth, precise, motivated: slow push-ins on the product, controlled orbit /
turntable moves, locked hero shots. Nothing shaky or improvised.

## Texture and Material
Immaculate surfaces, crisp reflections, clean specular highlights, no grain, no
haze. Every material reads as high quality and intentional.

## Prompt Anchors
- "bright high-key commercial lighting, soft even fill"
- "product-forward composition, generous negative space, clean seamless background"
- "crisp specular highlights, premium surface reflections"
- "smooth slow push-in" or "controlled turntable orbit"
- "shallow depth of field isolating the product, no grain"

## Avoid
Dark low-key mood, heavy grain, cluttered busy backgrounds, handheld shake,
gritty texture, distracting environmental storytelling that pulls focus off the
product.
