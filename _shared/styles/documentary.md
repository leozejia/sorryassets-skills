# Documentary

## Identity
Naturalistic, observational realism with available light and unobtrusive
framing. Use for authentic, grounded, testimonial, or reportage pieces where
believability matters more than polish.

## Palette
Neutral, true-to-life color. Minimal grading; natural white balance. Muted
rather than saturated; whatever the real environment provides.

## Light
Available / motivated light: window light, overcast sky, practical lamps.
Moderate ratio (3:1–4:1), soft on faces. No obvious studio setup; the light
looks found, not designed.

## Composition and Lens
Observational framing — medium shots, over-the-shoulder, room for the subject
to exist naturally. Slightly imperfect framing reads as authentic. Normal lens,
natural depth of field. Interview subjects placed with looking room.

## Camera Movement
Handheld with subtle organic motion, or locked-off on a tripod for interviews.
Reactive rather than choreographed — the camera follows, it does not lead.

## Texture and Material
Real-world texture, natural skin, honest surfaces. Mild sensor noise acceptable.
No stylized rendering, no bloom, no heavy effects.

## Prompt Anchors
- "observational documentary look, available natural light"
- "neutral true-to-life color, minimal grading"
- "handheld with subtle organic motion" (or "locked-off tripod interview framing")
- "medium shot, natural depth of field, looking room"
- "authentic real-world texture, natural skin tones"

## Avoid
Neon, heavy color grades, dramatic low-key lighting, glossy stylization, showy
camera moves, symmetrical staged perfection.
